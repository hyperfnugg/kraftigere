# Family lists

Shared lists for a family: shopping, and later chores and other kinds. Every member of a family sees the same lists.

## Language

### Lists

**List**:
A collection of Entries belonging to at most one Family, which can have many Lists. Lists are never shared across Families.
_Avoid_: Shopping list (one List Kind, not the general concept)

**List Kind**:
What a List is for, such as shopping or chores. Every Entry on a List is of the List's kind. Shopping is the only List Kind today.
_Avoid_: List type, category

**Entry**:
One line on a List.
_Avoid_: Item, product, task

**Checked**:
The state of an Entry that has been dealt with, whether bought, done, or otherwise. A Family Member *checks off* an Entry and can *uncheck* it.
_Avoid_: Done, bought, completed

**Remove**:
Permanently deleting an Entry from a List. This is the only way an Entry leaves a List. It is distinct from checking it off.
_Avoid_: Delete, clear, archive

### People

**Family**:
The group of people who share Lists. It is always a family, never a team, group, or organisation.
_Avoid_: Group, household, team, tenant

**Family Member**:
A person, identified by their Google account, who belongs to a Family.
_Avoid_: User, member (without "Family")

**Admin**:
The single person who maintains Families and their Family Members. This is a system-wide role. No Family has its own head.
_Avoid_: Owner, head of family
